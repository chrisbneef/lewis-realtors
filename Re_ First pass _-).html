SOP: Review and Refine a Real Estate Landing Page/Lead Funnel for West Linn

Objective

Ensure the website/landing page accurately reflects the brand, highlights key local expertise, and routes leads to the correct resources and systems. This SOP helps a team member update page content, links, and lead magnets while keeping the page polished, specific, and conversion-focused.

Key Steps

 

1. Review the overall design and brand alignment 0:00

  • Confirm the page looks elegant, professional, and on-brand.

  • Check that the layout is visually balanced and easy to scan.

  • Identify any immediate design improvements before moving into content edits.

 

2. Update typography for brand consistency 0:14

  • Increase the size of the top fonts if they feel too small.

  • Replace fonts with ones that match the brand guide.

  • Verify the updated typography improves readability on desktop and mobile.

 

3. Strengthen local credibility and market stats 0:27

  • Keep the headline or proof points that highlight 23 years of local experience.

  • Display the $128 million since 2016 stat prominently.

  • Make sure these credibility markers are easy to find and support trust quickly.

 

4. Expand neighborhood-specific messaging 0:27

  • Add more West Linn neighborhood references where appropriate.

  • Prioritize Stafford and Hamlet area in the copy.

  • Ensure neighborhood mentions feel natural and relevant to the audience.

 

5. Confirm the page sections and local pros list placement 0:57

  • Verify the section intended for the B&I list with local pros is placed correctly.

  • Make sure the content block is clearly labeled and easy to understand.

  • Confirm the section supports local authority and community connection.

 

6. Organize buyer and seller lead magnet paths 1:10

  • Confirm the page includes separate paths for Buyer and Seller.

  • Add the appropriate lead magnets for each audience.

  • Include the following if available:

    • Moving/relocating from West Linn lead magnet

    • Moving to West Linn lead magnet

  • Place these assets where they best support conversion.

 

7. Verify destination links and page routing 1:25

  • Check whether the current links route to the same page or separate destinations.

  • Confirm the user journey is intentional and not confusing.

  • Update routing if needed so each CTA sends visitors to the correct next step.

 

8. Enhance the seller-side community and marketing message 1:32

  • Strengthen the seller section with messaging about community involvement.

  • Include references to marketing the community and using custom videos.

  • Make sure the seller value proposition is clear and compelling.

 

9. Add scheduling and workflow integrations 1:32

  • Confirm a Calendly link is available for the relevant section.

  • Verify the page connects properly to the existing Monday project system and CRM workflow.

  • Document whether the page should send leads into Monday, the CRM, or both.

 

10. Insert business contact and social links 1:57

  • Add the PO box if it is needed for the page.

  • Check the Instagram link and reconnect it if it is currently spammed or disconnected.

  • Test all contact and social links after updating them.

 

11. Review supporting market tools and value-add content 2:12

  • Keep the market reports visible and easy to access.

  • Highlight the home value feature as a useful lead-generation tool.

  • Confirm these resources are working and positioned to add value for visitors.

 

12. Finalize copy for clarity and specificity 2:26

  • Tidy the copy by making it more specific where needed.

  • Remove vague language and replace it with clearer, more targeted wording.

  • Do a final quality check to ensure the page feels polished and complete.

Cautionary Notes

  • Do not change brand fonts or layout without confirming they match the approved brand guide.

  • Avoid adding neighborhood references that are inaccurate or not relevant to the target market.

  • Verify all links, lead magnets, and integrations before publishing to prevent broken user journeys.

  • If Instagram remains disconnected, do not leave the broken link live.

  • Ensure any CRM or Monday workflow changes are documented so leads are not lost.

Tips for Efficiency

  • Make all copy and design updates in one review cycle before requesting final approval.

  • Use a checklist to confirm each CTA, form, and link is tested on desktop and mobile.

  • Reuse approved brand fonts, neighborhood language, and proof points across the page for consistency.

  • Prepare lead magnets and routing destinations in advance so the page can be updated without delays.

  • Keep a simple handoff note listing what was changed, what still needs fixing, and what requires approval.
