Are you sitting at the kitchen table thinking, "Do I list now, or do I wait until spring?" If that question has been circling for weeks, you're not alone. I call it real estate paralysis: the headlines feel loud, the seasons feel like they matter, and so you do nothing.

Let's take some of the noise out with real West Linn numbers.

## Where the market sits right now

On **October 12, 2026**, there were **142 houses** for sale in West Linn, with a **median asking price of $1,009,088**. That's asking prices, what sellers are listing for, not what homes are selling for.

Prices haven't swung much this year. Our monthly snapshots put the median asking price at **$974,950 in late July**, **$1,025,000 in mid-August** and **$1,009,088 in October**. That's a fairly steady market, not one that's falling out from under sellers.

## The number that matters most: time on market

Here's something worth understanding. The median house **still for sale** in October had been listed for **75 days**. That sounds slow.

But look at the homes that **actually sold**. In June 2026, the most recent month of closed sales we've loaded from RMLS, **50 detached homes sold** in West Linn at a **median price of $854,500**, and the **median sold home spent just 12 days on the market**.

Both numbers are true, and they tell one story. Homes that are priced and prepared well tend to move quickly, and they leave the active list fast. The homes that sit are the ones that keep the active-listing number high. Time on market is less about the season and more about whether a home is priced for the buyers who are actually out there.

## Selling well in the rainy season

If you do list this fall, a few things carry extra weight here:

- **Price it right from day one.** The June numbers show what happens when a home lands where buyers expect it.
- **Get ahead of the rain.** Clean gutters, check drainage and fix anything that shows water. Buyers notice it, especially in October.
- **Light it up.** Short days make photos and showings darker. Bright bulbs and open blinds make a real difference.
- **Plan around the holidays.** Showings slow in late December, so timing your launch matters.

## Now or spring?

There's no single right answer, and I'd never push you either way. Waiting can make sense if your home needs work that's easier in dry weather or your next move isn't ready. Listing now can make sense if you're ready, your home shows well, and you'd rather not wait another six months.
