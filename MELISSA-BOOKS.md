## Process frameworks worth reusing on the site

**Seller: Every Step to Sold (10 steps)** — Meet with your agent; Prepare to sell;
Imagery (professional photo, video, drone, floor plans); Marketing launch; Showings;
Receive and review offers; Negotiate terms; Inspections and appraisal; Repairs; Closing.

**Buyer: The Home Buying Journey (10 steps)** — Buyer consultation; Solidify funds
(pre-approval); Refine your search; House hunting; Write an offer; Accepted offer
(earnest money, disclosures, title, due diligence); Inspections and appraisal; Loan
documentation; Signing and closing; Keys.

**Buyer pledge highlights** — dedicated partner, transparent communication and honest
advice, advocate for your best interests, impartial feedback after tours, demonstrate
you're a serious buyer, lifelong access as a trusted resource, client appreciation
program after the sale.

**Valuation language (validates the new site copy)** — "A preliminary comparative
market evaluation can be created, however for a more serious professional opinion an
on-site visit is usually required with all the home details and features taken into
account along with the neighborhood and community."

**Pricing philosophy** — "Price Like A Pro": pricing correctly the first time; the
problems with listing too high (exclusion from searches, perception of a distressed
property, appraisal risk). What determines price: market condition, comps, inventory,
features, exposure, location, condition. What does NOT: what you paid, what online
sites think, what you need to make.

**Marketing** — "Full Spectrum Marketing": custom video tours as crafted stories,
targeted social, agent-to-agent networking; "In a market where buyers shop online
first, we make sure your home gets the attention it deserves." Education-through-video
is a pillar ("Our videos are a reflection of who we are").
