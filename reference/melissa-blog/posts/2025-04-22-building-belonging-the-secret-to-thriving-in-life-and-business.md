# Building Belonging: The Secret to Thriving in Life and Business

If you're planning to sell or buy a home in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, or Mid-Willamette Valley there's one factor often overlooked in the decision-making process—community. For me, real estate goes beyond bricks and mortar. It’s about lifestyle, connection, and creating a legacy. That’s why I always ask: What does community mean to you?

As someone who moved here in my early twenties—alone and far from home—I know firsthand that living your best life starts with where (and with whom) you plant your roots. I struggled so much with finding my people that I moved back to Australia twice. That longing for connection? It’s what shaped how I serve my clients today.

If you’re a seller wanting to position your home strategically or a buyer searching for your next chapter, understanding how your neighborhood fosters community should be at the core of your decision.

## Why Community Should Guide Your Real Estate Choices

The real estate market in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA and Mid-Willamette Valley is full of beautiful homes, but homes alone don’t create a fulfilling lifestyle. The people, the spaces, and the sense of belonging around your home do. When I work with clients, I'm not only helping them buy or sell a house—I'm helping them build a life they’re proud of.

Here’s how community plays a pivotal role in where you choose to live:

- A Sense of Belonging and Connection
I get it—relocating can feel overwhelming. Like I said, “It took me some time to find that [sense of belonging], but it really was a make-it-or-break-it.” That moment when you don't just live somewhere, but feel like you’re part of it—that’s when your house becomes home.

A strong community provides:

- Welcoming neighborhoods where people know your name
- Shared spaces that promote connection—parks, walking trails, local events
- Unspoken support networks for life’s moments, both big and small
These aren’t just “nice-to-haves”—they allow you to live your best life.

- Stronger Personal and Business Networks
As a full-service real estate professional, I always have my radar up for amazing local businesses. I’m committed to supporting and promoting small business owners in our region because they shape the culture of where we live.

When choosing your next neighborhood, ask:

- Is there a farmer’s market you love visiting on Saturdays?
- A yoga studio that hosts community classes under the trees?
- A local coffee shop where people gather to talk and dream?
These moments create more than daily routines—they create meaningful life rhythms.

- Stability, Security & Peace of Mind
We’ve seen it—events like wildfires, ice storms, or power outages bring people together in ways nothing else can. “Knowing your neighbors, knowing that you're looking out for each other,” becomes more than a perk. It becomes a lifeline.

Homes nestled in neighborhoods with strong traditions of neighborliness evoke:

- Greater safety for kids, pets, and families
- A shared sense of trust and reliability
- Emotional resilience during challenging times
It’s not just about where you live—it’s about who’s living beside you.

- Opportunities to Give Back
One thing I love most about our communities in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA and Mid-Willamette Valley is the opportunity to make an impact. Through my real estate practice, I pay it forward to the very people who serve our neighborhoods every day—health professionals, teachers, firefighters, police officers, and military families.

One way to assess a great community is to ask:

- What causes are supported here?
- How do neighbors show up for each other?
- Is there a group or event I can get involved in?
Because when you give back, it anchors you in something meaningful and magnifies a sense of purpose.

- Living Your Best Life Starts with Connection
Whether you're a seller crafting the best story for your property or a buyer hoping to find your next peaceful haven, remember this:

In my real estate family, it's never just about the transaction. It's about creating a lifestyle that supports who you are becoming.

I encourage you to reflect:

- What do you love about your current neighborhood?
- What are you hoping to find in your next move?
- Where do you feel a true connection with people and place?
Let’s work together to not just find a house—but your place. Because better together is how we were meant to live.

When you’re ready to make a move that aligns with your values, family, and future—reach out. I’m here to help you build a life rooted in connection.
