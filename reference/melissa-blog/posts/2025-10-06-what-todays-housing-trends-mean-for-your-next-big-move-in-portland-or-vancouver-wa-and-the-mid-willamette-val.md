# What Today’s Housing Trends Mean for Your Next Big Move in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley

As we move into the fall season, understanding the current real estate trends in the Portland, OR metro, the Mid-Willamette Valley, and the Vancouver, WA metro areas becomes essential for anyone considering their next big move. Whether you’re planning to sell and transition to another property or you’re looking to purchase a home that fits your evolving lifestyle, I’m here to break down the data so you can make informed decisions.

I’m Melissa Shaw with Lewis Realtors, and with over 20 years of experience in navigating this dynamic market, I’m committed to bringing you the knowledge and strategy you need to make your next real estate move confidently.

Let’s find out what’s happening in each key market area this August and how to interpret the data for your goals.

## Portland, OR Real Estate Market: August 2025 Snapshot

The Portland, OR metro area continues to show signs of stability and healthy appreciation.

Highlights from August:

- New Listings: 2,476 homes hit the market, down from July but up year over year by 530 homes
- Pending Sales: Up month over month with 2,275 homes under contract
- Closed Sales: Holding steady at 2,021 for the month
- Average Sale Price: $620,000, a slight dip of $1,300 from July but up over $55,000 compared to August 2024
- Median Sale Price: Year over year gain of $45,000, slight $5,000 dip from July
- Days on Market: Increased to 62 days
- Inventory: Down slightly to 3 months
What this means: “We are still appreciating at a very solid and stable rate,” and while the numbers might feel slight, they show a neutral market that gives both buyers and sellers a balanced playing field.

If you’re selling in Portland, OR, make sure your listing is “priced the cream of the crop.” Buyers are becoming more selective, and pricing paired with a strategic marketing presentation matters.

## Mid-Willamette Valley: Polk & Marion Counties Market Insights

The Mid-Willamette Valley has its own rhythm, and this month’s data tells a story of opportunity for those entering the market, especially first-time buyers and investors.

MARKET STATS FOR AUGUST:

- New Listings: Down slightly to 265 homes
- Pending Sales: Stable at 240
- Closed Sales: 186 homes, down month over month but up by 55 from last year
- Average Price: $462,000+
- Median Price: Just under $450,000, steadily increasing year over year and month over month
- Days on Market: 60 days
- Inventory: The highest of the three regions at 3.9 months
If you’ve been waiting for more options or a bit more breathing room to explore, this is a “prime opportunity for you to secure a home at a lower price point with more selection and choices.”

## Vancouver, WA Real Estate Market: August 2025 Update

Clark County is holding its own and reflecting a strong market, even with minor dips.

Vancouver Area Quick Stats:

- New Listings: 789 homes entered the market, down from July but up compared to 2024
- Pending Sales: Down month over month by 44, yet year over year up by 150
- Closed Sales: Fairly consistent numbers
- Average Sale Price: $609,400, down $11,000 from July, up $40,000 year over year
- Days on Market: 62 days
- Inventory: Slight increase to 3.6 months
This area has seen some impressive movement. One of my recent listings in Vancouver, WA had “over 40 showings and three offers in just three days.” That ratio tells us a lot. Buyers are looking, but they’re also discerning.

As a seller here, you “really need to be coming to market with your best.” Pricing, presentation, and marketing strategy are everything.
