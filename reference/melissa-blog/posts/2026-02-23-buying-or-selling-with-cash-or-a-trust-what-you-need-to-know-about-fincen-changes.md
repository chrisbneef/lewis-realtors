# Buying or Selling with Cash or a Trust? What You Need to Know About FinCEN Changes

In recent real estate discussions, you might have encountered the term "FinCEN." If you’ve ever wondered what it means and how it impacts you, you’re in the right place. Here at Lewis Realtors, my aim is to provide you with the critical information you need to make informed real estate decisions. Understanding FinCEN is particularly important for buyers and sellers involved in cash transactions, or those dealing with property transfers through a trust or LLC.

## What is FinCEN?

FinCEN stands for the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, a bureau of the US Department of Treasury. Designed to prevent illicit money laundering, FinCEN is becoming an integral part of real estate transactions. Historically, cash transactions involving LLCs or trusts have been vulnerable to deceptive practices regarding fund sources. The Residential Rule introduced by FinCEN seeks to address these issues.

## Who does FinCEN affect?

If you are purchasing a home with cash, or through an entity like an LLC or a trust, these changes will directly impact you. According to the National Association of Realtors, cash transactions accounted for 26% of all transactions last year. This significant share of the market means that over a quarter of homes sold might be affected by these new rules.

## Key Dates to Remember

The new rule set by FinCEN is scheduled to roll out on March 1st. Although this has been delayed previously, staying informed is crucial as the date approaches. More documents will be needed, and the closing process may take longer due to required disclosures. Rest assured, the objective of FinCEN is to maintain the credibility of the real estate industry, not to make your transactions more cumbersome.
