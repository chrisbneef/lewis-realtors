# Buying a Home in Today’s Market: What More Inventory Means for Buyers

If you’ve been watching the market and hearing that inventory is higher, you might be wondering what that actually means for you if you're thinking about buying a home this year.

I’ve had this conversation many times with clients who want to make smart decisions for their next move. And here’s the truth: more inventory means more opportunity. It means we are in a more stable market where you have time to think, compare, and choose the right home without being rushed or pressured.

## More Homes Means More Options and Less Pressure

In this market, we are seeing:

- More homes available
- Longer days on market
- More room for thoughtful decision-making
That combination creates a major shift for buyers. You no longer have to compete with ten other people or make a fast decision just to get in the door.

"You can slow down. You can make time to really think through your decisions."

You get to be more selective. You can take the time to ask whether the home, the neighborhood, and the investment are truly right for you and your family.

## You Have Negotiation Power Beyond the Price

One of the biggest advantages in this market is the ability to negotiate. And I’m not just talking about the purchase price.

We can look at seller credits, closing cost contributions, and repair negotiations. There are many points in a transaction where we can advocate for you.

I think of a first-time homebuyer I worked with recently. During the inspection, he was ready to take on all the repairs himself. He had some help lined up and was prepared to tackle everything. "I looked at them and said, you know, I know that you can do this, and I know that you have the resources through family and friends, but it’s still going to cost you time and money. So what if we ask the seller, in lieu of these repairs, for a credit toward your closing costs?"

He was surprised and said, "I didn’t know we could do that." And that’s what I love most, being able to guide clients through options they didn’t even know were available.

## You Have Time to Make the Right Choice

This market gives you time. And time is powerful.

You can ask yourself the important questions:

- Is this the right home?
- Is this the right community?
- Is this the right long-term investment?
"You're not missing red flags like you might have if you were rushed."

For years, buying a home felt like an uphill battle and a competition. But now, it is more about making the right choice at the right time for you.

"Imagine that a second or even third showing before you decide."

The likelihood is high that the home will still be available tomorrow. That’s a big shift from the fast-moving markets of the past.
