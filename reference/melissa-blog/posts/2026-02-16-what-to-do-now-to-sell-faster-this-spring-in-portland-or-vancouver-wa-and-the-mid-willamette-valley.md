# What to Do Now to Sell Faster This Spring in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley

As we’ve been enjoying a stretch of unexpected sunshine, it’s easy to start feeling like spring is around the corner, even if it’s technically still a few weeks away. This extra daylight always gets me thinking about fresh starts, planting, and preparing our spaces to really enjoy the season ahead.

Whether you're looking to improve your home's experience for your own enjoyment or considering selling in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, or the Mid-Willamette Valley, getting ahead of spring home maintenance can make all the difference.

## Why Early Planning for Spring Maintenance Matters

I’ll be honest, if you wait until spring officially hits, you might already be behind. The truth is, by the time spring rolls around:

- The contractors you want are already booked
- Landscaping crews are stretched thin
- You’ll be last in line for the updates you wanted months ago
That means you’re either stuck doing it yourself or facing delays that push your improvements well into the summer. The key is to start planning and scheduling now.

## What Can You Do Right Now?

Early prep makes spring feel seamless. Even if the weather is still unpredictable, there are plenty of ways to start refreshing your home inside and out before spring truly arrives.

Let's break it down:

Interior Updates to Start This Month

If it’s raining (again), tackle some valuable indoor refreshes:

- Declutter your home to create more space and light
- Spring cleaning in high-traffic areas makes a huge visual impact
- Touch up paint on high-use walls or trim
- Fix squeaky hinges and floors for a quieter, smoother experience
These small touches improve not only your day-to-day life but also show prospective buyers that a home has been loved and maintained. “When people walk through it, they sense that this home has really been taken care of.”

Exterior Maintenance That Pays Off

As soon as we get those sunny days, get a head start outside:

- Inspect your roof for damage and schedule any needed repairs
- Clean your gutters and downspouts to avoid water damage
- Power wash moss buildup on steps, sidewalks, or pavers
- Prune shrubs and trees while they’re still dormant
- Upgrade your outdoor lighting, especially at the entry areas
- Sweep porches and walkways to refresh your approach
Small changes like refreshing your curb appeal or clearing off outdoor debris can transform the feel of your home’s outdoor space.

## Consistent Care = Homes That Sell Faster

Over the years, I’ve seen firsthand that homes that undergo regular seasonal maintenance show better and sell faster.

“I can walk through a home and immediately tell if it’s been immaculately maintained,” and buyers feel that too. It builds trust and confidence, and that’s priceless when your property goes on the market.
