# When Real Life Meets Real Estate: Discovering Strength Through Personal Struggles and Community Impact

Have you ever felt like you're living a double life? I know I have. There have been seasons where I struggled to balance my personal life, especially my health challenges, with my professional role as a full-service real estate professional in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley. For a while, I questioned how I could bring the two worlds together in a way that felt real, compassionate, and helpful to others.

Then it hit me. I already do.

The challenges I face every day in my health have quietly shaped the way I show up for others. Whether it's helping clients navigate a complex home sale in a shifting market or supporting someone who is going through a life transition such as divorce, loss, becoming an empty nester, or moving closer to grandchildren, I'm not just guiding them as a REALTOR. I'm walking beside them, because I understand what it’s like to face the unknown.

## The Real Story Behind Lewis Loves

Today, I’m writing this from outside the studio, raw and unfiltered, a blanket draped around me, on my way to receive 120 injections in my cervical spine and shoulder.

This is just one part of many routine treatments for Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS), a connective tissue disorder that causes ligament laxity and chronic pain. I usually keep this part of my life private, but it’s central to the reason I created Lewis Loves.

Lewis Loves is more than a blanket initiative. It was created as an umbrella of care, rooted in empathy and a desire to serve others, especially those navigating health struggles or deep life transitions. These blankets are tangible expressions of compassion and a reminder that someone cares.

## How My Health Journey Connects to My Real Estate Practice

It might surprise some to find a connection between chronic illness and real estate, but for me, they’re deeply intertwined.

When you’ve walked through pain, the kind that’s invisible to most, you start to develop a deeper sensitivity toward others.

That translates directly into how I serve my clients:

- Empathetic listening: Whether you're moving out of your longtime family home or buying your dream retreat property, I hear what’s not said.
- Out of the box solutions: I’ve had to learn creative problem-solving to navigate life with EDS. That same creativity helps me craft innovative marketing strategies and deal structures for clients.
- Above and beyond service: Chronic health challenges have taught me to appreciate patience, persistence, and personalized care which I bring into every transaction.
My calling as a trusted advisor isn't just about property. It's about legacy, healing, growth, and finding peace of mind in one of the most significant financial and emotional decisions of your life.
