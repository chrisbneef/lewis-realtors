## A BRIEF HISTORY OF HOMEOWNERSHIP IN THE U.S.

In the 1800s, the U.S. landscape was primarily rural, with just 47% of Americans owning homes. Back then, home purchases were entirely market-driven, with no government assistance programs like today's.

Fast forward to the 20th century, and things began to change significantly, especially during and after periods of economic crises. For instance:

* The Great Depression (1930s): This era left countless families homeless. But it also marked the birth of government programs aimed at helping Americans achieve homeownership and reducing foreclosures.

* Post-World War II: The U.S. saw a boom in new construction and suburban development. By 1969, nearly 64% of Americans owned homes, driven by growing prosperity and federal assistance programs.

## HOW 2008 SHAPED TODAY’S HOUSING MARKET

Anyone who lived through the 2008 housing crisis won’t soon forget its impact. It changed everything. I witnessed firsthand how devastating it was for families and our communities.

But positive change emerged from the crisis. New safety regulations were introduced to stabilize the housing market, rebuild trust, and prevent another financial collapse of that magnitude. Fast-forward to 2023, and we’re still feeling the ripple effect of those changes today.

## WHAT TO EXPECT IN THE HOUSING MARKET MOVING INTO 2025

Let’s talk about the future of real estate so you can feel confident about your next steps.

Here’s the good news: indicators point to relief on the horizon.

* New Construction Boom: Just like in the post-World War II era, we’re seeing an increase in new construction permits, which means more inventory is coming.

* Federal Assistance Programs: Programs aimed at helping buyers and stabilizing the market are expected to expand, making it easier to secure financing.

## WHY HOMEOWNERSHIP IS STILL A SMART INVESTMENT

I know affordability is a valid concern—it’s something that’s at the top of everyone's mind. But here’s what I want you to remember: homeownership is one of the most reliable ways to build generational wealth and create financial stability for your family.

## Here’s why it’s worth pursuing:

* Asset Growth: Unlike renting, homes appreciate value over time, helping you build equity.

* Legacy Building: A home isn’t just a financial investment; it’s a place where lifelong memories are made and family roots are planted.

* Stability: Studies show that kids who grow up in stable home environments tend to earn higher grades, reinforcing the long-term benefits of homeownership.
