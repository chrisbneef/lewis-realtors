# When It Feels Impossible: How One Client Found Her Path to Homeownership

Are you sitting there thinking, "I'm never going to have a home. I don’t have a down payment. I'm barely making ends meet "? You’re not the only one feeling this way, and I want you to hear this clearly: there is a way forward. It can feel almost impossible when you look at today's market. Home prices are high, interest rates haven’t dropped the way we hoped, and it’s easy to compare today’s housing costs with what your parents or grandparents paid for their homes.

But here’s the truth, owning real estate remains one of the most powerful ways to build long-term asset wealth and security for your family.

That’s not just a talking point. It’s a lived experience I’ve seen over and over again in my two decades as a real estate broker here in the Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley areas.

## Real Estate Is Still the #1 Asset Builder

Even in a market with rising prices and less-than-ideal interest rates, home values continue to increase over time. That’s one of the few constants in an ever-changing market.

“Because the fact remains: the rates haven’t really come down substantially. The fact remains that home prices are continuing to go up. The fact remains that owning real estate is your number one asset wealth builder.”

For you, that likely means reimagining your entry point into the real estate market. You may not be buying your dream home right out of the gate but just getting into the market can be the biggest step toward long-term financial growth and stability.

## A Story That Might Sound Familiar

Let me tell you about a recent client of mine a young woman who was convinced homeownership was out of reach.

She didn’t think she had saved enough. The idea of affording a mortgage seemed way too big. But we had a conversation, ran the numbers, and found that she actually could be a homeowner. Her joy during the process was contagious.

But then it hit:

“We got that call 24 hours prior to closing that the lender couldn’t fund the loan. There was this red tape… and she was devastated.”

I felt that devastation with her. But I always say, where there’s a will, there’s a way. So I got to work contacting multiple lenders. And we found one—one who could make it happen, with a program that allowed us to close in just 21 days.

Today, she’s living in her own home.

## Yes, There Are Down Payment Programs Available

There are actual low and no-down-payment options out there today—options that didn’t exist even just a year ago.

Some examples:

- 0% down payment programs: These are real, and not just for first-time buyers
- Seller-paid closing costs: In many cases, I can negotiate with the seller to help cover those
- Creative financing options: If you have unique credit or income situations, let’s explore tailored programs
Maybe your path to ownership looks a little different. Maybe it’s a starter home or an investment property you can build from. What matters most is starting.
