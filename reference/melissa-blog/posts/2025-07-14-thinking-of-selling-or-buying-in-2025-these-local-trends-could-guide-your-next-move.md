## Portland, OR Real Estate Market – May 2025

The Portland real estate market is showing some interesting shifts, especially if you're a seller contemplating listing this summer.

Key Data Points:

- New Listings: 3,651 (up from May 2024)
- Pending Sales: 2,299 (down year-over-year)
- Closed Sales: 2,018 (down year-over-year)
- Average Sales Price: $624,100 (up from $616,700 in April 2025)
- Median Sales Price: $569,500 (climbing from $558,500 in May 2024)
- Days on Market: 55 days (down from 62 in April 2025)
- Inventory: 3.3 months (a healthy bump from last year)
While pending and closed sales are down compared to May 2024, we’re seeing appreciation in both average and median prices and that’s a key indicator that value is holding strong.

Even more encouraging for sellers is the decreased market time from April, which tells us the right listings specifically, move-in ready and updated homes in highly sought-after neighborhoods are still drawing strong interest and moving quickly.

## Polk & Marion Counties Real Estate – A Different Story

If you’re in the Mid-Willamette Valley areas (like West Salem, Independence, or Stayton), the trends take a different turn.

Key Data Points:

- New Listings: 319 (down from May 2024)
- Pending Sales: 241 (down year-over-year)
- Closed Sales: 207 (also down from 2024)
- Average Sales Price: $539,500 (continuing month-over-month growth)
- Median Sales Price: $477,000 (up from $458,600 last May)
- Days on Market: 54 (down from 57 in April)
- Inventory: 3 months (down from 3.5 in April, but still elevated year-over-year)
We are definitely seeing slower activity in terms of transactions here, but sellers should note: both average and median sales prices are still growing month-over-month and year-over-year. That’s especially helpful if you're planning to list in a market that prioritizes quality presentation.

## Clark County, Vancouver, WA Market Update

Let’s head over the river into Southwest Washington and see how Clark County compares to Portland and the Mid-Willamette Valley.

Key Data Points:

- New Listings: 986 (up from 943 in May 2024)
- Pending Sales: 616 (slightly down from 655 in May 2024, but up from April)
- Closed Sales: 534 (steady from April’s 535, down from 556 in May 2024)
- Average Sales Price: $634,900 (holding strong, slight dip from April’s $644,000)
- Median Sales Price: $559,000 (up from $542,300 last May)
- Days on Market: 50 (down significantly from 65 in April)
- Inventory: 3.4 months (rising from 2.9 in April and 2.6 in May 2024)
Clark County is showing more price stability than anywhere else in our tri-county analysis. While the number of sales is relatively flat, market time has dropped substantially from 65 to 50 days, and values remain solid. For both buyers and sellers in Vancouver, WA, the takeaway here is clear: a well-prepared home is still highly competitive and getting offers quickly.
