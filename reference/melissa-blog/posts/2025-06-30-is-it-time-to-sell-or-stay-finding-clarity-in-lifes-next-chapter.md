# Is It Time to Sell or Stay? Finding Clarity in Life’s Next Chapter

You know that your home isn’t working for you the way it used to—but making the decision to move feels like a weight on your shoulders. If you're entering what I like to call the “third trimester of life,” this phase tends to bring a desire for clarity, peace, and a lifestyle that aligns with your future.

You may not be stuck because of the market or money. You're stuck trying to make the right decision.

I’ve worked with many homeowners in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley neighborhoods who are in this exact stage of life. What they crave most isn’t another data point or sales pitch, it’s a thought partner. Someone who can help them unpack why they feel stuck and what pieces they need to get unstuck.

If you're in that place today, you're not alone. And I’m here to share how we can find clarity together.

## When Your Home No Longer Fits Your Life

Over time, the home that once held years of memories might now feel empty or burdensome. Kids are growing up, grandkids are on the horizon, and winter months in the Pacific Northwest increasingly don’t spark joy.

That’s often when I hear from folks in neighborhoods like Lake Oswego, Camas, or the West Hills who feel caught between two desires:

- Wanting something different for their future
- Needing to feel 100% sure before letting go of what’s familiar
In these conversations, it's rarely just about square footage or equity. What most people are really seeking is clarity.

## It’s Not That You Don’t Know—It’s That You Need Confirmation

I recently sat down with a woman facing this exact scenario. She knew intuitively what she wanted. She was ready to move but the financial pieces hadn’t fully come together in her mind.

She told me, "I just need to know I'm making the right decision."

So we worked through it together. Not with pressure, but with compassion, strategy, and honesty.

The decision-making process isn’t about selling or staying. It’s about confident and informed choices that align with your goals and your values—especially when you're planning for the next chapter of your life.

## The Questions That Unlock the Right Direction

Whether we stay or go, we all want peace of mind with our decisions. Sometimes it just takes the right questions to get there:

- Where do you see your life in the next five years?
- What does your ideal day-to-day look like?
- What’s not working about your current home?
- What legacy do you want your home or assets to support?
When my clients sit with these questions, the path often becomes clearer. Their answers lead us to the next step, whether that’s scheduling a market analysis, running through financial projections, or connecting with trusted tax professionals for long-term planning.

## It's About More Than Just Selling Homes

Real estate is personal, and so is the decision to make a move later in life. I'm not here to just list houses. I’m here to be your trusted advisor who brings out-of-the-box ideas and deep listening to every conversation.

That’s why I believe in building relationships, not transactions. Whether you decide to move or stay, the result should be clarity and peace of mind.

If you're stuck in limbo, unsure if this is the right time or the right move, let's talk. We’ll get real about what’s holding you back and explore what it would take to start living your best life in this new season.
