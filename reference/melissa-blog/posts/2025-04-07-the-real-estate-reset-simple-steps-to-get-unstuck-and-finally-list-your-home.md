# The Real Estate Reset: Simple Steps to Get Unstuck and Finally List Your Home

Are you thinking about selling your home but feel stuck on where to begin? You are not alone. Many homeowners look around and wonder, where do I even start? With all the demands of daily life, starting can be the hardest part. But taking small, manageable steps can set the momentum in motion, helping you move toward your real estate goals with confidence.

## The Clutter Challenge: You're Not Alone

Did you know that one in four people have a room in their home that’s not being used because it’s filled with "stuff"? And one in two homeowners have garage space they never use simply because it’s cluttered. If that sounds familiar, don’t worry. The good news? Decluttering doesn’t have to be overwhelming. By breaking it down into small tasks, you can make real progress toward preparing your home for sale—without feeling buried under the process.

## How to Start Decluttering Today

The key to getting started is to take it one step at a time. You don’t need to tackle the biggest project first. Instead, begin with something small and manageable.

- Choose the Easiest Starting Point
- Pick a space that feels least overwhelming to declutter. It might be a single drawer, your pantry, or even your refrigerator.
- Make it part of your routine. Try going through one kitchen drawer after dinner each night to build momentum.
- If you prefer to see bigger progress, choose an easier room to complete first before tackling larger areas like the garage.
- Create Three Piles: Keep, Donate, Toss
- Keep: Essential items you still use and need. Consider packing an "open first" box with must-haves for your next home, like medications, linens, kids' essentials, and important documents.
- Donate: Items in good condition that no longer serve you. Research local charities such as Veterans programs, Goodwill, Salvation Army, or St. Vincent de Paul, many of which offer pickup services.
- Toss: Let go of broken or unnecessary items without guilt. Decluttering in this way is empowering. It frees up space not just in your home, but in your mind as well.
- Set a Timer for Digital Decluttering
Decluttering isn’t just physical—it’s digital too. Unsubscribe from emails that no longer serve you and clear out your inbox. Set a timer for 15 minutes a day or a few times a week to eliminate unnecessary digital clutter. You’ll be surprised how much mental clarity this simple step can bring.

- Let Go of the Guilt
Holding onto unused items out of guilt doesn’t serve you. The goal is to simplify your space and make way for the future home and lifestyle you’re working toward. Letting go will give you back time, energy, and peace of mind.

## Ready to Take the Next Step?

Decluttering is a powerful first step toward preparing your home for sale. Whether you’re thinking of selling soon or simply want to create a more organized space, these simple strategies can help you move forward with clarity and confidence.
