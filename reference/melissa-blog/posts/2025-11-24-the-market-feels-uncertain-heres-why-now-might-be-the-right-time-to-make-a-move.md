# The Market Feels Uncertain: Here’s Why Now Might Be the Right Time to Make a Move

If you're feeling overwhelmed by the current real estate market in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, or the Mid-Willamette Valley, you're not alone. Between news headlines and rising uncertainty, it’s easy to fall into what I call real estate paralysis, where you want to make a move but don’t even know which direction to go.

Whether you're thinking about selling your current home, buying your next one, or both, I get it. It can feel confusing, even exhausting at times. Maybe you’re wondering, "Can we even make a move right now?" or "Should we wait and see what happens with the market?" These are valid questions, especially for families who are rooted in their community and are considering big lifestyle transitions.

But I want to offer you something that sometimes feels missing from day-to-day real estate conversations: hope.

## Why Now Might Actually Be a Great Time to Act

Let’s talk about what’s actually happening right now in the market and why both buyers and sellers can find real, tangible opportunities. Despite the doom-and-gloom narrative you might hear in the headlines, the truth is, we are in a much more balanced market than we’ve seen in a long time.

Here’s what that could mean for you:

Buyers: You have more leverage to negotiate terms that work for your lifestyle and goals, especially in homes around the $1M+ price point.

Sellers: You have time to prepare to thoughtfully present your home to get the best possible result.

Your next move doesn’t have to be a rushed response to the market. Instead, it can be a well-planned strategy grounded in clear numbers, honest conversations, and thoughtful marketing.

As one of my clients told me recently, “We had no idea it was even possible. But Melissa made it happen with options we never thought to consider.”

## A True Story: From Frustrated Sellers to a Fantastic Outcome

Let me share one of my favorite client success stories.

Not long ago, I was working with sellers preparing to list their home. We had everything in motion, but once we hit the market, a major issue surfaced: the foundation wasn’t sound. Financing would no longer be an option for any buyers. You can imagine how devastating that felt after all the time and effort they’d already invested.

Naturally, my clients felt discouraged. And honestly, it would have been easy to stop right there. But that’s not how I work.

One of the core values of my business is this: Where there’s a will, there’s a way and "Can’t never do anything". So I went to work. I got creative, leaned on my network, and started looking for the right buyer for this home.

Long story short? We found a cash buyer who saw the value in the home as-is and my clients were elated.
