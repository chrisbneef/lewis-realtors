# Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley: What June’s Numbers Reveal About Your Next Best Move

## Portland, OR Market: Balanced and Brimming with Opportunity

The Portland, OR Metro market continues to offer opportunity with increasing inventory and stable activity. We’re seeing homes sitting on the market a little longer, which makes standing out more important than ever.

Quick Stats for June 2025 – Portland, OR Metro:

- New Listings: 3,096 (up year-over-year)
- Pending Sales: 2,267 (slight increase)
- Closed Sales: 1,988 (slightly down)
- Average Sales Price: $634,400 (slightly down)
- Median Sales Price: $570,000 (up year-over-year)
- Days on Market: 51 (slightly up)
- Inventory: 3.6 months – the highest we’ve seen in years
“Sellers, if you're thinking of selling, it's even more important now that your home stands out with a strategic approach,” I always remind my clients. You don’t have to redo everything but preparation and positioning are everything if you want to reduce the time on market and maximize your return.

## Polk & Marion Counties: A Transition Period for Strategic Buyers

In Polk and Marion Counties, we're seeing some significant changes, lower price points compared to recent months, and high days on market. For first-time homebuyers or investors seeking long-term asset growth, this could be a golden opportunity to break into the market.

June 2025 Market Snapshot – Polk & Marion Counties:

- New Listings: 322
- Pending Sales: 219 (slightly down)
- Closed Sales: 192 (slightly down)
- Average Price: $462,900 (down from May’s $539,500)
- Median Price: $426,400 (down, both month-over-month and year-over-year)
“If we look at the median and the average sales prices year-over-year, we're even down year-over-year,” which doesn’t happen often in today’s market. That’s why it may be smart to act quickly if this area is on your radar.

## Vancouver, WA & Clark County: The Hottest Market Right Now

Clark County, WA including Vancouver, WA is surging. Demand remains strong and inventory is active, which is fueling consistent home price growth. For sellers who’ve been on the fence, this could be the moment to move forward confidently.

Key Market Data – Clark County (SW Washington):

- New Listings: 907 (up year-over-year)
- Pending Sales: 658 (slightly up)
- Closed Sales: 579 (up year-over-year)
- Average Sales Price: $647,300 (up)
- Median Sales Price: $574,900 (up from $535,000 in June 2024)
- Days on Market: 49
- Inventory: 3.3 months
“Clark County is the hottest market right now… so if you're a seller in that area, it really might be the optimal time to sell your home.”
