Let’s break down what you really need to know and do to buy your first home or your next home in today’s market.

## Step 1: Evaluate Where You Are Financially

Before thinking about neighborhoods or walking into open houses, start with a clear financial snapshot. This groundwork is essential not just to see what you qualify for, but to feel empowered in your buying decisions.

Here’s what to look at:

- Savings: What do you have set aside for a down payment, closing costs, and a cushion for early homeownership?
- Budget: What monthly payment feels manageable without stretching you too thin?
- Credit Score: Get your free yearly credit report and review it. Are there errors or subscriptions you forgot about? This is your moment to tidy it all up.
You’d be surprised how many people pay for services they didn’t even know they signed up for. A few minutes on your bank and credit card statements might free up hundreds a month of money better served building your future.

## Step 2: Get Paired With the Right Lender

This step often feels overwhelming, but it doesn’t have to be. You don’t need to have all the answers, you just need to connect with someone who knows the right questions to ask. Whether you’re in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, the Mid-Willamette Valley, or planning a real estate move across the country, I can help match you with a trusted lender that fits your needs.

Lenders today offer so many more options than just the conventional or FHA loans:

- First-time home buyer programs
- Creative investor loans
- Programs for Baby Boomers looking to leverage equity in their current home
There’s no one-size-fits-all approach, but there is a loan program that’s right for you. The key is working with a lender who sees your unique path clearly and can help you navigate it.

Whether you're looking in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, or the Mid-Willamette Valley, we align your needs with the current market to uncover opportunities that work for you.
