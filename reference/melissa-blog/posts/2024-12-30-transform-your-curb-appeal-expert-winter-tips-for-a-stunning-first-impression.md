# Transform Your Curb Appeal: Expert Winter Tips for a Stunning First Impression

## FRESHEN UP YOUR HOME'S WINTER CURB APPEAL: TIPS FOR THE RAINY SEASON

Have you ever driven by a house and thought, “Wow, I’d love to come home to something that is inviting every day”? Your home can give you that feeling, too—whether you’re planning to sell or just want a more welcoming space when you pull into your driveway. Winter can make everything feel a little gloomier here in the Pacific Northwest, but with a few simple updates, you can add serious curb appeal, even during the darker, wetter months.

I’m Melissa Shaw, and with my years in real estate, I’ve seen what works to make homes shine—especially in the winter season here in Portland, OR, and Vancouver, WA. Whether you want to sell in 2025 or "freshen up" your home's exterior, I’ve got you covered.

Here are my favorite tips for enhancing your home’s curb appeal this winter:

## LIGHTING: ILLUMINATE YOUR BEST FEATURES

Good lighting is an easy way to make your home pop, even on overcast days.

* Use warm, soft uplighting to highlight architectural features or trees in your yard.

* Add LED lights along pathways to create a cozy, functional, and beautiful glow.

* Consider Wi-Fi-enabled smart lighting for easy control, keeping cords neat, and avoiding tripping hazards.

## CLEAN UP THE EXTERIOR

Rainy winter days often leave homes looking dull and neglected, but a little maintenance goes a long way.

* Clean your gutters: Overflow can lead to unsightly stains and promote mold or moss growth.

* Pressure wash exterior surfaces: Driveways, walkways, and decorative bricks will look fresh and squeaky clean.

* Keep the roof tidy: Ensure your roof is free of algae, moss, or debris (no need to climb up yourself—hire a professional).

## ADD PACIFIC NORTHWEST GREENERY

Embrace our region’s natural beauty by incorporating evergreen elements to brighten your front entrance.

* Use neutral-toned pots and fill them with greenery to complement your home’s exterior.

* Add wreaths or bold planters to make your front porch welcoming and vibrant.

## MAINTAIN YOUR LAWN & LANDSCAPING

Yes, it’s winter—everything’s wet and cold—but ignoring your yard can make it look worse when spring rolls around.

* Keep your lawn mowed and well-drained to avoid soggy, muddy patches.

* Refresh garden beds with a layer of mulch to prevent weeds and give a polished look.

* Trim back overgrown plants and maintain walkways and edges for a clean finish.

## REIMAGINE THE FRONT DOOR

Give your entryway a facelift—this is the focal point of your home’s curb appeal.

* Consider a fresh coat of paint in a bold but inviting color like red or navy blue.

* Add a seasonal wreath or greenery to make it look cozy during cold, gray months.

## WEATHERPROOF FOR WINTER

Staying ahead of the elements is crucial in our damp climate.

* Ensure any outdoor electricals, like lighting, are weatherproofed for safety and longevity.

* Use outdoor-rated materials for décor pieces to avoid damage from rain, moisture, or frigid temperatures.
