# Serving Others Is at the Heart of What We Do

## The Power of Unity in the Community

We've all had those moments when life knocks us flat. One of the most life-changing moments in my life came when I became a single mom, lost my health, and even lost the ability to walk.

At that time, I embodied pure self-reliance. If it was going to be, it was up to me. But this season of complete vulnerability taught me something that changed the trajectory of my life and how I serve others through real estate, community, and beyond.

This experience gave birth to the heart behind Lewis Loves, a movement, not just a message, to bring unity back into the community.

## From Self-Reliance to Interconnected Strength

When I look back on those difficult days, what shaped me most wasn’t just the healing or recovery. It was the people who came alongside me with no expectation and with open hearts.

- They paid my electric bill so I could keep the lights on.
- They bought Christmas presents for my kids when I couldn’t.
- They drove me to appointments when I couldn’t walk or drive.
I couldn’t pay them back. But they weren’t in it for repayment. They were in it to love, serve, and uplift. And I learned something essential during that chapter:

“Unconditional love was shared in service, it was shared without any expectation of anything that I could do for any of these people.”

Those deep experiences gave me a renewed purpose to model that same unity and support in every part of my life, including how I serve clients, support local communities, and collaborate with partners.

## Why Unity Matters in Real Estate and Beyond

Service, trust, and community aren’t buzzwords in my work. They are what anchor everything I do as a full-service professional and trusted advisor in the Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley real estate market.

I work with individuals and families who are navigating big transitions, selling homes where memories were made, buying homes for a new chapter, or investing in real estate to build wealth and legacy. And in every situation, I bring more than expertise. I bring understanding, connection, and care.

This is deeply personal to me. Because I know how much strength there is in togetherness. Together, we:

- Solve complex real estate problems with creative strategies
- Build personal relationships that prioritize your values
- Uplift local businesses and create strong neighborhoods
- Help you live your best life, with community behind you

## Lewis Loves: More Than a Name, It's a Message

Lewis Loves didn’t just spring out of a catchy phrase. It was born from the reality that together, we are truly better. It’s a reminder that we can cultivate a sense of unity in our local communities through small daily actions, through real estate done with purpose, and through supporting each other without agenda.

"Unity in community is about us all coming together as a unified whole, serving each other, because we are better together. And you can't tell me anything different because I'm living proof."

## This Is What I Stand For

If you're someone who's seeking not just a transaction but a people-centered experience, you're looking in the right place.

This isn’t about houses, it's about homes, memories, legacies and doing daily life together. It’s about people. It’s about unity. It’s about family. It’s Lewis Loves.
