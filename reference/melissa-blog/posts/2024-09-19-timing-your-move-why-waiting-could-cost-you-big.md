# Timing Your Move: Why Waiting Could Cost You Big

## When Is the Right Time to Make Your Move? Understanding the Cost of Waiting in Real Estate

Moving from one home to another isn't a decision most people make on a whim. It's an intricate process influenced by numerous factors including emotions, family impacts, financial decisions, and market uncertainties. So, when is the right time to make your move? Let's delve into the crucial concept of "The Cost of Waiting" in real estate.

## The Emotional and Financial Challenges of Moving

Few people wake up one day excited to pack up their lives and move. As I often say, "Does anyone really wake up going, 'Man, I can't wait to move today?' Probably not."

Moving encompasses more than just logistics; it's about the emotional attachment to your home and the potential ripple effects on your family. You re also faced with significant financial decisions and market uncertainties, such as current mortgage rates. With rates still higher, many homeowners find themselves asking, "Is it the right time to move?"

THE IMPORTANCE OF ACTING DESPITE HIGHER RATES

It s easy to understand the hesitation when contemplating a move. Higher rates create confusion about when that "perfect time" might truly be. However, history has shown us that waiting for rates to drop can be detrimental. Take 1971, for example. If you d waited for lower rates before making your move, you wouldn t have been able to act until 1993.

Imagine missing 22 years of potential real estate appreciation. Between 1971 and 1973, home prices quadrupled. By waiting, you miss out on significant wealth-building opportunities.

UNDERSTANDING MARKET PREDICTIONS

Given current forecasts, waiting could lead to missed opportunities. In quarter 3 of 2024, we re anticipated to see a 25% increase in real estate transactions, and in quarter 4, we expect a 40% increase. This deviates from traditional market patterns over the last 30 years.

Key insights and local market information from your trusted real estate professional can be crucial. Can you imagine if you didn't know this information and you waited until next spring to make your move?

THE IMPACT OF ENTERING THE MARKET NOW

More buyers are expected to enter the market due to pent-up demand and shifting demographics. Many sellers are also looking to relocate closer to family or purchase second homes for their retirement years. This influx of buyers can impact your ability to secure the best property at the best price. Increased competition and anticipated appreciation in the forthcoming months are significant factors to consider.

THE COST OF EQUITY LOST

One of the most compelling reasons to act now is the potential equity gain or loss. There's a striking difference in net worth between homeowners and renters, largely due to property ownership. According to a 2019 Federal Reserve study, homeowners have a median net worth of $255,000, while renters have just $6,300. This disparity underscores why homeownership remains a fundamental wealth-building tool.

As someone who restarted my real estate journey in my 40s, I can personally attest to these benefits. It's never too late to start. Your decisions today can cultivate wealth not just for your present, but for future generations.

Procrastination can cost you significantly in equity and potential growth. Assess your unique situation, consider current market predictions, and act before the anticipated market surge.
