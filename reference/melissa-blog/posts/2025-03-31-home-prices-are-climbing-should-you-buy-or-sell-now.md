# Home Prices Are Climbing – Should You Buy or Sell Now?

Are you waiting on the sidelines, unsure about the housing market? Wondering if now is the right time to buy or sell in Portland, Mid Willamette, OR or Vancouver, WA? You're not alone. Many homeowners and buyers are observing mortgage rates and market trends, trying to make the right decision.

I hear this all the time: "I was thinking about moving last year, but I hesitated. Now home prices have gone up!" If this sounds familiar, this market update is for you. Let’s break down the real estate market trends in Portland, Mid-Willamette, OR and Vancouver, WA for February 2025 compared to February 2024. This data will help you make informed decisions about your next move.

## Portland, OR Market Trends: February 2025 vs. February 2024 – New Listings Are Down

Inventory remains tight in Portland, OR.

- February 2024: 2,805 new listings
- February 2025: 1,930 new listings
That’s a decrease of almost 1,000 homes year-over-year. Fewer homes on the market mean continued competition among buyers.

Pending & Closed Sales

Even with fewer listings, homes are still selling.

- Pending Sales (Feb 2024): 1,742
- Pending Sales (Feb 2025): 1,648
- Closed Sales (Feb 2024): 1,284
- Closed Sales (Feb 2025): 1,428
Closed sales increased in 2025, which tells me serious buyers are still active and making moves despite economic uncertainties.

Home Prices Are Rising

Property values continue to appreciate in Portland.

- Average Sale Price (Feb 2024): $576,900
- Average Sale Price (Feb 2025): $613,100
- Median Sale Price (Feb 2025): $538,300
That's a substantial increase in home values year-over-year. If you were waiting for the “perfect” time, consider how much pricing has already shifted.

Market Time & Inventory

- Days on Market (Feb 2024): 68 days
- Days on Market (Feb 2025): 79 days
- Months of Inventory (Both Years): 3.2 months
While a 3-6 month inventory level typically indicates a neutral market, Portland still has areas where homes are selling fast—especially move-in-ready, well-updated properties in desirable locations.

## Vancouver, WA Market Trends: February 2025 vs. February 2024

New Listings & Pending Sales

Like Portland, new listings dipped in Vancouver, WA.

- New Listings (Feb 2024): 602
- New Listings (Feb 2025): 532
Pending sales have also slightly declined:

- Pending Sales (Feb 2024): 569
- Pending Sales (Feb 2025): 475
With fewer listings and pending sales, Vancouver’s market remains steady but competitive.

Closed Sales & Home Prices

Homes continue to appreciate in Vancouver.

- Closed Sales (Feb 2024): 402
- Closed Sales (Feb 2025): 419
- Average Sale Price (Feb 2024): $557,200
- Average Sale Price (Feb 2025): $597,800
- Median Sale Price (Feb 2025): $530,300
That’s nearly a $40,000 price increase over one year!

Market Time & Inventory

- Days on Market (Feb 2024): 64 days
- Days on Market (Feb 2025): 77 days
- Months of Inventory: 3.2 months (2024) → 3 months (2025)
Certain Vancouver neighborhoods are still seeing strong demand, faster sales, and competitive offers depending on the location and condition of the home.
