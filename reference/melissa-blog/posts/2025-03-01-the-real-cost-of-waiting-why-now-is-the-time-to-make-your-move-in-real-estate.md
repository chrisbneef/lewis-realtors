## What I Learned: 3 Reasons Waiting Could Be Costing You

During this event, I discovered three critical reasons why delaying your real estate decision could have financial consequences:

1. Home Prices Are Still Shifting

Even in a changing market, home values in sought-after neighborhoods in Portland, OR and Vancouver, WA continue to hold strong. If you wait too long, you could be priced out of your ideal home—or miss an opportunity to maximize your home’s value as a seller.

2. Interest Rates Affect Your Buying Power

Mortgage rates fluctuate, and even a small increase can impact how much home you can afford. Locking in a rate now could mean securing a lower monthly payment compared to waiting.

3. Inventory Is Moving Faster Than You Think

Many buyers assume they have plenty of time to decide, but in competitive areas, well-priced homes are still selling quickly. If you’re thinking about upgrading, downsizing, or purchasing an investment property, waiting could mean missing out on the best options.
