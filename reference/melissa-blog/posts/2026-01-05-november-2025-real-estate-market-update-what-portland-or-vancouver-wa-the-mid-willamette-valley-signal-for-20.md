# November 2025 Real Estate Market Update: What Portland, OR Vancouver, WA & the Mid-Willamette Valley Signal for 2026

The housing landscape across Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley is shifting. As we edge closer to 2026, many sellers and buyers are asking: What kind of market are we stepping into?

## Portland Metro: Balancing Out, with Steady Demand

In Portland, OR, the November 2025 numbers reflect a market that is recalibrating, not retreating. Here's what stood out:

- New Listings: Dipped slightly to 1,437
- Inventory: Rose to 3.8 months, up from 3.1 in October
- Pending Sales: Increased to 1,628, showing demand is still active
- Closed Sales: 1,520, reflecting longer decision cycles
- Median Sales Price: Held even at $535,000
- Average Sales Price: Softened slightly to $599,700
- Average Days on Market: Increased to 72 days
Buyers are still shopping, just more intentionally. Sellers who take the time to present their homes well and understand current buyer behavior are the ones seeing results. "The big picture is that Portland, OR is moving in a positive, stable direction," and that stability rewards well-prepared clients.

## Mid-Willamette Valley: Signs of Normalization

The Mid-Willamette Valley specifically Polk and Marion Counties near Salem is showing the clearest signals of a normalizing market:

- New Listings: Held steady at 201
- Pending Sales: Slowed to 177
- Closed Sales: 171, matching seasonal expectations
- Median Sales Price: Increased to $447,000
- Average Price: Rose to $485,200
- Inventory: Climbed to 4.1 months
- Average Days on Market: Increased to 69 days
Even with a seasonal slow-down in activity, pricing remained strong. This supports long-term confidence, a great sign for families looking to build asset wealth through real estate in this area.

## Vancouver, WA: Strong Demand and Price Resilience

The Vancouver, WA and greater Clark County market continues to show strong signals of a stable, high-performing area:

- New Listings: Decreased modestly to 437
- Pending Sales: Rose to 498
- Closed Sales: Slightly lower at 406
- Median Sales Price: Jumped to $581,000
- Average Price: Rose to $630,000
- Inventory: Climbed to 4.1 months
- Average Days on Market: Increased modestly to 71
Price trends upward even, as supply builds a clear indication that buyers see long-term value in Vancouver, WA, regardless of broader market adjustments. “Clark County is absorbing inventory, but it is not affecting their prices,” reflecting an area with strong roots and continued appeal.
