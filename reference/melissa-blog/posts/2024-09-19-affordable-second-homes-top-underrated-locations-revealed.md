# Affordable Second Homes: Top Underrated Locations Revealed

## Top Affordable Vacation Home Spots You Need to Consider

Are you mulling over where you might like to buy a second home but aren't quite sure which one might be the best fit? Today, I offer you six locations that you may not have thought of before, but are listed by the National Association of Realtors as top places, hidden gems, and more affordable options for your consideration.

## 1. Gatlinburg, Tennessee

Gatlinburg is known for the Great Smoky Mountains National Park, making it a haven for nature lovers. With a median sales price of approximately $320,000, Gatlinburg offers a blend of scenic beauty and affordability. Whether you're looking to hike, enjoy stunning views, or simply relax in a cabin, this spot has a lot to offer.

## 2. St. Augustine, Florida

St. Augustine boasts amazing historic charm. It's the oldest city in the U.S. and is rich with historical landmarks, beautiful beaches, and vibrant culture. The median price point here is around $365,000. If you're a history buff or just love a city with a story, St. Augustine might be the perfect spot for your vacation home.

## 3. Gulf Shores, Alabama

The Gulf Shores of Alabama offer beautiful beaches and a warm, welcoming community. With a median sales price of around $402,000, it's a slightly higher price point, but the stunning coastal views and relaxed lifestyle make it a worthy consideration.

## 4. Branson, Missouri

Branson is known for its entertainment and outdoor enjoyment. From live music shows to fishing and hiking, there's something for everyone. The median sales price in Branson is just under $300,000, making it an affordable option with plenty to offer.

## 5. East Stroudsburg, Pennsylvania

East Stroudsburg, nestled in the Pocono Mountains, is an outdoor enthusiast's dream. The area offers skiing, hiking, and beautiful mountain views. The median sales price here is also just under $300,000. It's a perfect spot if you're looking for a scenic and active lifestyle.

## 6. Whitewater, Wisconsin

Whitewater is a very scenic location with a charming, small-town feel. The median sales price is around $315,000. It's ideal for those who appreciate picturesque landscapes and a peaceful environment.
