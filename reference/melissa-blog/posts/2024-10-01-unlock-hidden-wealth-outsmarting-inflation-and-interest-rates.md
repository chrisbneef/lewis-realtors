## HOW REAL ESTATE HELPS BEAT INFLATION

One of the most compelling reasons to invest in real estate is its ability to act as a hedge against inflation. Consider this: if you had invested $10,000 in a down payment for a $100,000 home back in 1964, that same property would be worth approximately $323,000 by 1984, around $600,000 by 2004, and over a million dollars today! This incredible appreciation isn’t just coincidental. It’s a perfect example of how real estate prices outpace the devaluation of money over time.

## HOW TO SAVE ON INTEREST RATES WITH SIMPLE MORTGAGE STRATEGIES

Navigating high-interest rates? Don’t worry, I've got some practical tips for you:

- Extra Annual Payment: Did you know that by making just one extra mortgage payment per year, you could save over $220,000 in interest and shave seven years off a $500,000 mortgage at an 8% interest rate?
- Bi-Monthly Payments: If an extra annual payment sounds challenging, consider switching to bi-monthly payments. This effectively adds one extra payment per year without feeling the pinch.
- Monthly Principal Payments: Another option is to divide the extra annual payment amount over 12 months. This smaller, consistent addition to your principal each month will still yield significant long-term savings.
