## The Essential Guide: How to Pick the Perfect Pumpkin

Don’t settle for just any pumpkin! Here are my tried-and-true tips to help you pick one that’s festive, functional, and stays fresh all season long.

✔ Check the Stem

You want a stem that's either green or brownish. This means the pumpkin has been freshly harvested and hasn’t been sitting too long.

✔ Look at the Color

A deep orange hue is ideal. This color tells you it's ripe and ready, perfect whether you're using it for cooking or displaying it on your front porch.

✔ Evaluate the Shape

Think about your pumpkin’s purpose.

- Short and squat pumpkins: Great for baking and recipes.
- Rounded pumpkins: Best for carving and family fun.
✔ Feel the Skin

Run your hands over it and look for smoothness if you want a clean carving canvas. If you prefer character and textures, choose one with bumps. But be cautious. If your thumb finds any soft spots, it's probably going to rot quickly. Time to move on to the next one.

## Pro Tips from the Pumpkin Patch

When you're heading out to select your seasonal prize, here are a few extra pro tips to make your visit more enjoyable:

- Wear gloves and boots: Fields in the Pacific Northwest can be muddy this time of year
- Go local: Farms with pumpkins still attached to their vines offer the freshest picks
- Cut smart: Trim the stem a few inches from the base to keep your pumpkin fresher for longer
