# From Dream Home to Best Life: How Your Next Move Starts with One Question

Have you ever caught yourself daydreaming and thought, “If I could live in any type or style of home, what would it be?” It's a powerful question, one that goes beyond square footage or architectural style. In my experience as a real estate professional helping families and professionals throughout Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley, that simple question often reveals much more than just design preferences.

It uncovers lifestyle goals, values, and even deep desires for what’s next in life. Whether you’re looking to downsize, upsize, relocate, or reimagine your lifestyle altogether, the type of home you’re drawn to say a lot about the life you want to create.

## Why Your Ideal Home Style Matters

We all reach different seasons of life, kids move out, priorities shift, and suddenly the home that once fit perfectly doesn’t quite serve you anymore. I recently sat down with a woman who had lived in her beloved two-story home for 30 years.

“She loved it,” I shared, “but her needs weren’t so much liking those stairs anymore.”

It’s a familiar story, especially in communities like Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley, where families often outgrow or age out of their spaces. It's a reminder that “the type and style of home that you're dreaming of actually tells you a lot about what you're really looking for in a home and the lifestyle you're actually wanting to achieve.”

Your home should reflect how you live today and how you aspire to live moving forward.

## Questions to Ask Yourself When Reimagining Home

When considering a home transition, here are a few introspective questions that help guide your real estate choices:

- What part of my home no longer supports the way I live?
- If I could design my home from scratch, what elements would I keep? What would I change?
- Am I envisioning one floor, a high-tech setup, a cozy bungalow, or maybe something closer to nature?
- What daily lifestyle am I hoping this home will support?
Maybe you're dreaming of a smart home that simplifies your busy days or a home closer to your grandkids or even a retreat where winter feels far from Portland rain.

## Common Lifestyle Shifts I See in Our Community

Throughout my two decades in real estate, I’ve noticed consistent lifestyle transitions that indicate it might be time to consider a move:

- Empty nesters whose homes suddenly feel a bit too big (or too noisy with all those stairs)
- Professionals and executives who want a space better aligned with their remote work or travel lifestyle
- Active families looking for more walkability, access to trails, or outdoor amenities close to schools and modern conveniences
- Grandparents-to-be or newly minted grandparents who want to spend more quality time near family while avoiding the winter drizzle
Whether you're leaning toward single-story ease, modern design for aging-in-place, or smaller foot traffic to prioritize travel, the key is aligning your next home with your current and future goals.
