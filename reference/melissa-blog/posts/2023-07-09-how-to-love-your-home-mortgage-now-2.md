# HOW To LOVE Your HOME & MORTGAGE NOW.

Embarking on the journey of homeownership is an exciting venture, and with a little knowledge and strategy, you can truly fall in love with your mortgage and home. In this article, we'll explore two key aspects that can make your homebuying experience achievable in todays market of higher rates, looking for homes that appraise for higher than listed and being aware of your maximum seller concessions based on your loan type.

- Hunting for Homes that Appraise Higher: One of the ways to ensure you love your mortgage and home is by strategically searching for properties that have the potential to appraise for higher than their listed price. Here are some tips to help you in this pursuit: Research Comparable Sales: Conduct thorough research on recent sales in the area. Look for properties with similar features and square footage to gauge the potential appraisal value.
- Consider Upcoming Developments: Investigate any planned developments or improvements in the neighborhood. The prospect of increased property values due to positive changes in the community can contribute to a higher appraisal.
- Home Improvements and Renovations: Focus on homes that may have undergone recent renovations or improvements. Upgraded features can positively impact the appraisal value.
- Consult with Real Estate Professionals: Collaborate with knowledgeable real estate agents who can provide insights into the local market trends and guide you toward properties with high appraisal potential.
- Understanding Maximum Seller Concessions for Your Loan Type: Seller concessions can play a crucial role in your homebuying process. Being aware of the maximum concessions allowed for your specific loan type can help you navigate negotiations effectively. FHA Loans: If you're utilizing an FHA loan, be aware that the maximum seller concessions are generally capped at 6% of the home's sale price. Understanding this limit can help you structure your offer within acceptable parameters.
- Conventional Loans: Conventional loans typically have more flexibility in negotiations. While there is no strict percentage cap on seller concessions, lenders often consider factors like your credit score and down payment amount.
- VA Loans: For those using VA loans, it's essential to know that there's no set limit on seller concessions. However, lenders may have their own restrictions, so it's crucial to communicate with your loan officer.
- USDA Loans: USDA loans may allow seller concessions up to 6% of the home's purchase price. Being aware of this limit can guide your negotiation strategy when making an offer.
Check with your local lenders for your seller concession allowances specific to you.

- By strategically approaching your home search with an eye for properties that appraise higher and understanding the maximum seller concessions for your loan type, you can negotiate a great rate for yourself by increasing the purchase price on homes that appraise and securing a lower rate for yourself resulting in loving your home and mortgage.
