# Surprising Shifts in Buyer Activity: What Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley Homeowners Should Know Right Now

As we wrap up 2025 and move into 2026, I want to share not just the stats, but also some first-hand market insights. If you're a homeowner in one of Portland, OR or Vancouver, WA’s sought-after neighborhoods, or you're thinking of making a move, this Portland real estate market update gives you a clear picture of where things stand and where we're heading.

Let’s start with what I’m personally seeing with buyers in early 2026, and then we'll find out the official December 2025 numbers for the Portland, Salem, and Vancouver, WA areas.

## Early 2026 Market Shift: What I’m Seeing Now

Before we explore the December stats, I want to zoom in on something I'm seeing right now in January 2026.

We’re experiencing a shift in buyer activity and it's not just me. Conversations with other local real estate pros echo this same observation. There’s noticeably more movement. I've had an increase in buyer inquiries, and I’m actively working with more buyers than in previous months.

This activity is particularly strong at the mid $500,000 and below price points, which could suggest an early season push that we haven’t seen in a while. What’s most interesting is that the market feels a lot like 2019 not in terms of pricing or interest rates, but in inventory levels and how long homes are sitting on the market.

I’ll be keeping a close eye on this and will share January’s numbers as soon as they come in. It will be telling to see if this real-time uptick in buyer behavior is reflected in pendings.

Portland Metro Area: How December 2025 Closed Out

The Portland Metro numbers for December reflected the usual seasonal dips while still showing market balance and strength.

Here’s what stood out:

- New Listings dropped to 1,005, which is typical for December
- Inventory inched up year over year to 2.9 months but dropped from November’s 3.8 months
- Pending Sales totaled 1,234
- Closed Sales jumped to 1,633, a notable increase month over month
Pricing remained stable:

- Median Sales Price was $535,000 — up year over year, unchanged from November
- Average Sales Price was $596,400 — also holding steady
- Average Days on Market came in at 81 days, showing homes are taking a bit longer to sell
This data tells a clear story. Even though homes are staying on the market longer, deals are happening when sellers focus on smart pricing and proactive preparation.

## Salem Area (Polk & Marion Counties): Signs of Recalibration

If you have property interests south of Portland, OR, the Polk and Marion County area continued to show signs of recalibration for December.

- New Listings were down to 164 from November’s 201, but up slightly year over year
- Pending Sales rose to 171, consistent month over month
- Closed Sales fell to 135, down from 204 a year prior
What stands out here is pricing resilience:

- Median Sales Price increased to $450,000
- Average Sales Price dipped just slightly to $468,000
We also saw inventory expand year over year to 3.7 months (down from 4.1 the previous month)

Homes spend an average of 77 days on market, which gives buyers a bit more leverage and provides sellers with critical feedback on what’s needed to capture interest.

## Vancouver, WA & Clark County: Key Inventory Drop

In Clark County, the story continues with variation and a notable drop in inventory.

- New Listings came in at 353, down from 437 the previous month
- Pending Sales dropped to 382, reflecting both monthly and yearly decreases
- Closed Sales increased to 518, showing that buyers are still committing, they’re just taking longer
Here’s where things get interesting:

- Median Sales Price rose to $550,000 — a slight increase year over year, slight dip month over month
- Average Sales Price was $610,000 — same trend
But the biggest standout? Inventory hit the lowest point all year at just 2.8 months, confirming tight supply. Average market time landed at 83 days.
