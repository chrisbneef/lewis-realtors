## Shorter Days on Market

One of the highlights of our local markets is the relatively short days on market. Currently, homes in Southwest Washington and Portland, OR are averaging 34 to 36 days on market. This is significantly lower than the national average of 105 days, showing that our areas remain highly desirable.
