## What’s Inside the Fall Home Season Guide?

This free guide is meant to bring a little joy into your season. It’s filled with ideas and simple touches that make a big difference, including:

???? Cozy Front Porch Decor Ideas

Your front door sets the tone for everything that happens inside. Whether it’s adding mums, seasonal wreaths, or layering textures with oversized blankets and lanterns, these ideas will elevate your home’s outdoor welcome. "Think about those things that you would love to come home to. The first thing you see when you pull up to your driveway."

???? Vanilla Pear Sparkler Recipe

Hosting doesn’t have to be complicated. Invite a few close friends over, make a refreshing seasonal drink, and enjoy some intentional conversation. The vanilla pear sparkler is simple, unique, and captures all the best flavors of fall.

"It doesn't have to be a lavish event, it could just be a small group of people something that encapsulates the fall season."

????️ Fall Home Maintenance Checklist

Avoid last-minute surprises when the real cold hits. The checklist includes important seasonal reminders like:

- Testing and replacing smoke & carbon monoxide detector batteries
- Checking weather stripping around windows and doors
- Cleaning gutters and downspouts
- Scheduling your furnace inspection
Trust me, "you don’t want those last minute, in the middle of the night, wake ups of that chirp-chirp." Take this chance now to get ahead of home maintenance with just a few easy tasks.
