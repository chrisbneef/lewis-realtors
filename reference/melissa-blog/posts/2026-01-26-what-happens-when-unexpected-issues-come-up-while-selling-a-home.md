# What Happens When Unexpected Issues Come Up While Selling a Home

Even with the best preparation, surprises still happen

After 23 years in real estate and more than a thousand home inspections, I can tell you that unexpected home inspection issues are more common than most people think. It is not a sign of failure or poor planning. Sometimes, things come up that no one could have predicted.

I recently worked with a seller who had done everything right. The home was beautifully presented and ready to welcome the next buyer. Then an issue came up during the inspection. One we could not have seen coming. It was a moment that could have unraveled the entire deal.

## What Really Makes the Difference When Things Go Sideways

The truth is, problems are not always avoidable. What matters most is how your real estate professional responds when they do show up.

Here is what I have found to be essential for sellers in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley who want to keep their transaction on track:

Problem-Solving with a Plan: Real estate is not black-and-white. When a surprise shows up, the ability to think quickly, calmly, and creatively becomes the key to keeping the deal moving.

Trusted Relationships: In the example above, a contractor I have worked with for years agreed to handle the repairs and be paid through escrow. This was not a favor. It was the result of years of working with people who believe in helping each other out when it matters most.

Strategic Escrow Solutions: There is more than one way to resolve repair costs or delays. In the right hands, escrow can be used as a tool to protect both the seller and the buyer, especially when there are budget concerns.

Knowing Who to Call: Sometimes it takes more than just phone calls or emails. I have had to go straight to the city and county officials to help keep a transaction moving. It takes persistence, respect, and clarity to get things done when challenges arise.

## Why This Matters Even More in Today's Market

In neighborhoods where homes are priced between 800,000 and 1.5 million, buyers are informed and thoughtful. They want to feel confident in their decision. If a deal falls apart, it can create questions and concerns for future buyers.

When a home goes back on the market, people begin to wonder what went wrong. Even if the issue has been resolved, it can still affect how buyers approach the property. That is why it is so important to work through surprises and keep things moving forward the first time.

## There Is Almost Always a Solution

I truly believe that every challenge has a way forward. In my role, I listen first, then step in to find the best way to address what is happening. Sometimes that means getting creative with financing. Sometimes it means asking for help from someone in my network. But there is always something that can be done.

This mindset is one of the reasons I have maintained a zero percent sales fail rate over two decades in this business, even when the national average is between 6 and 15 percent.

## Thinking About Selling Your Home

If you are planning to sell in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, or the Mid-Willamette Valley, or even just starting to explore the idea, know that you do not need to have all the answers. What you need is someone who is prepared to support you when something unexpected comes up.

With the right people in your corner, there is always a path forward.
