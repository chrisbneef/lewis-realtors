# Feeling Stuck? This is What Helped Me Find Hope, Action, and a Fresh Start

There’s a growing conversation around the mood in and around Portland, OR. Researchers studying within a 20-mile radius of the city recently found a concerning pattern: people are feeling isolated, overwhelmed, and unsure of their future, leading to a general sense of hopelessness. As someone who has lived through market crashes, personal loss, and financial instability, I understand this feeling deeply. But I’m also here to say, it doesn’t have to stay that way.

Whether you’re navigating a real estate decision, a personal transition, or just trying to regain your footing, I want to share some tangible steps that helped me and my clients move forward. This perspective comes not just from my professional experience as a long-time real estate advisor in Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley, but also from my personal journey.

## Why Hope Matters More Than Ever

One thread that ties so many struggles together, whether financial, personal, or emotional is housing instability. The rising cost of living, increased mortgage rates, shifting markets, and emotional disconnection can leave even the most grounded folks feeling overwhelmed.

I’ve been there. In 2010, like so many others impacted by the aftermath of the 2008 housing crash, I lost my home. I was in the business at the time and still didn’t feel immune to fear or uncertainty. It wasn't until 2018, supported by my now-husband and my community, that I stepped out of fear and into belief again. That transformation started with one small but powerful thing: Mindset.

- Renew Your Mind
“Renewing your mind” was the first major shift that pulled me out of that dark space. It starts with reframing what you feed your thoughts. The media can easily pull you into worry or negativity. But every time I chose who I surrounded myself with, what I read, and what I believed about my situation. I found a little more ground under my feet.

Here’s what helped:

- Limiting negative input
- Seeking uplifting and real conversations
- Paying attention to what I could control, not what I couldn’t
- Asking the key question: “What can I do right now?”
Reframing from “I can’t” to “What can I do today?” creates powerful momentum. It’s not about ignoring reality, it’s about choosing to be active within it.

- Take Just One Step
When everything feels unmanageable, start small. Take one positive step forward, call a trusted friend, walk into that open house you’ve been curious about, or yes, call someone like me for a conversation.

One step breeds momentum. Momentum builds confidence. And confidence births belief. Even the smallest success rewires your brain to keep going.

In real estate, I see this happen every day. Whether you're downsizing, upsizing, planning a move to be closer to grandkids, or navigating life transitions, that one call often opens doors (literally and figuratively) to a new chapter.

- Believe a New Life Is Possible
"You are not stuck. You are not alone."

When I reflect on my own journey, I see proof that it’s truly possible to restore your future. That new life, that home that feels right again, it might feel far away now, but believe me, with the right mindset, meaningful action, and a supportive community, it’s reachable.

Whether this season has been hard because of financial burdens, personal loss, or health challenges or maybe you’ve just noticed that you’re not feeling as energized by your current surroundings, start by believing something better is possible.
