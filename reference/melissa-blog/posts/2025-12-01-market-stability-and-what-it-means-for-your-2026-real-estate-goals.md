## What’s Happening in the Market Right Now?

Despite what you might see in the headlines, Portland, OR, Vancouver, WA, and the Mid-Willamette Valley are showing consistency. That kind of stability empowers you to make smart, strategic moves with clarity.

Here’s a look at the latest data from October 2025 across key areas:

Portland, OR Metro

- New Listings: 2,366 (slightly down from September’s 2,498)
- Pending Sales: 1,988
- Closed Sales: 2,066 (slightly up from September)
- Average Sales Price: $606,000
- Median Sales Price: $540,000
- Days on Market: 69 (vs. 62 in September)
- Inventory: 3.1 months (up from 2.9 months in September, down from 3.8 last year)
We’re seeing well-priced, well-presented homes continue to sell. As I often say, “Buyers are willing to pay for a home when a home stands out and meets value.”

Mid-Willamette Valley (Salem, Polk & Marion Counties)

- New Listings: 255 (down from 286 in September)
- Pending Sales: 220
- Closed Sales: 229 (closed deals are up, even as we head into colder months)
- Average Sales Price: $470,400
- Median Sales Price: $440,000
- Days on Market: 54 (down from 64 in September)
- Inventory: 3.1 months
This region mirrors Portland’s trends with slightly more negotiating room and consistent pricing, creating a neutral, stable environment for both buyers and sellers.

Vancouver, WA Metro (Clark County)

Just north of Portland, Vancouver continues to appeal to downsizers, empty nesters, and those seeking tax advantages.

- New Listings: 655 (vs. 648 in September)
- Pending Sales: 527
- Closed Sales: 530
- Average Sales Price: $622,900
- Median Sales Price: $539,300
- Days on Market: 68 (up slightly from 65)
- Inventory: 3.6 months — highest among the 3 regions
Buyers here can take advantage of more inventory and more choices. Whether you’re an investor or a family looking for the right fit, this balance offers opportunities for strategic negotiation.
