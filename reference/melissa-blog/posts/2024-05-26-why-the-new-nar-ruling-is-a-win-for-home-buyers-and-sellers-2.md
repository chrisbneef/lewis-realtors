# Why the New NAR Ruling is a Win for Home Buyers and Sellers

## How the NAR Settlement Will Improve Your Real Estate Experience

The real estate market is about to undergo significant changes with the proposed NAR (National Association of Realtors) Settlement. As a seasoned realtor with over 20 years in the industry, I m excited to share how these changes will benefit both buyers and sellers. Let's dig into what this means for you and how it will enhance your real estate journey.

## The Importance of Raising Industry Standards

Over the past two decades, I ve witnessed numerous shifts in the real estate landscape, from the 2008 crash to the COVID-19 pandemic, which brought about unprecedented market conditions. During COVID-19, the market dynamics changed drastically, with homes selling rapidly and often with minimal effort from realtors. This surge revealed a decline in the skill level within our industry, as the high demand made selling homes relatively easy.

The new NAR Settlement is set to change this by elevating industry standards and ensuring transparency between consumers and realtors. This ruling is designed to hold realtors accountable, requiring us to demonstrate our value and what we bring to the table for our clients.

## Benefits of the NAR Settlement for Consumers

Here s why the proposed NAR settlement is great news for you:

- Enhanced Transparency: The ruling promotes greater transparency between buyers, sellers, and real estate agents. This means you will have a clearer understanding of what to expect and how we can help you achieve your real estate goals.
- Improved Professionalism: By raising the standards, realtors are encouraged to continually improve their skills and knowledge, ensuring that you receive top-notch service.
- Better Service Quality: With this ruling, realtors are motivated to provide the best possible service, focusing on your needs and ensuring your real estate experience is smooth and successful.
Reflecting on the challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic, I recognized the continued importance of offering exceptional service. Over a year ago, I began implementing growth within myself and my business to ensure that my clients receive the highest quality service. The proposed NAR Settlement aligns perfectly with this commitment, reinforcing my dedication to providing outstanding support to my clients.

As we move forward, you ll notice changes in the industry as state laws are adjusted to conform to the new guidelines. These changes will bring about a more robust and transparent process, ensuring that your real estate transactions are handled with the utmost professionalism.
