## Yes, You Still Need an Inspection

One might wonder about the necessity of inspections, especially in new construction. Here's a piece of advice: never skip them. Inspections are your safeguard, ensuring that every nook and cranny (seen and unseen) of your potential new home is in perfect condition. After all, it is a brand-new home. This means the standards are much higher than a resale home as far as condition. Inspections also serve as a vital punch list to give the builder representative at your orientation potentially saving you from unforeseen issues (crushed sewer lines for example) & saving you money that could affect your investment in the short and long run.

## Understanding Complex Contracts

The process of buying a new construction home involves navigating through contracts that can be a lot more complex than traditional purchase and sale agreements in resale homes. These documents are not just paperwork; they're the blueprint of your purchase that is contractually enforceable. My role is to decode these contracts for you, ensuring you're fully informed and comfortable at every stage of the process. In certain situations, I might also suggest consulting with an attorney to ensure your interests are thoroughly protected.
