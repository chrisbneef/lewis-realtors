# Job Hopping Drives Sellers Market

Did you know that approximately 37 million people in the U.S. switch jobs annually? That’s a staggering 3 million individuals every month. What’s more interesting is that the average age of a job switch is 39. This seemingly simple fact holds powerful insights for those thinking about selling their homes.

Hi, I’m Melissa Shaw, and I’ve been a trusted real estate advisor for over two decades. Today, I want to dig into why understanding this trend can help you, as a seller, prepare your home to attract the largest pool of potential buyers—millennials. Let’s break it down.

## MILLENNIALS: THE BIGGEST BUYING POWER IN U.S. HISTORY

At age 39, people are not only switching jobs; they’re also at peak home-buying age. Why does this matter? Because the millennial generation—the largest cohort of buyers in U.S. history—is currently in their late 20s to early 40s.

This group represents a massive wave of buyers entering (or moving up in) the housing market. Millennials are value-conscious and often motivated by significant life transitions, such as job changes, growing families, or wanting a better quality of life. If your home fits the needs of this demographic, you could be positioning yourself for an incredible selling opportunity.

## WHAT A JOB CHANGE REALLY MEANS FOR SELLERS

Career transitions often come with relocation or lifestyle upgrades. While not every 39-year-old changing jobs is also making a move, there is a strong connection between major career milestones and life decisions, like buying or selling a home. Here’s why:

* Relocation Needs: Switching jobs often results in a move to a new city or neighborhood. Portland, OR, and Vancouver, WA, are particularly sought after because they offer a blend of outdoor living, amenities, excellent schools, and a community feel—factors millennials prioritize.

* Upgrading Homes: Many buyers in their late 30s or early 40s are upsizing or looking for homes in neighborhoods that cater to active, family-centric lifestyles. If your home has great walkability, nearby hiking trails, or access to excellent schools, it’s a major selling point.

* Tight Inventory: With fewer homes on the market, your property has a higher chance of catching the attention of job-switchers who need to move quickly.

## LOWER INTEREST RATES AND MARKET TRENDS

Current market predictions suggest we could see a decline in interest rates in the coming months. Lower rates mean more buying power for millennials and other potential buyers. When you combine this with a low inventory market, as a seller, you’re sitting on an incredible opportunity.

These factors create a “perfect storm” scenario: increasing buyer demand (especially from millennials entering their prime buying years) and an environment where homes are poised to sell quickly and for competitive prices.
