## Simple Hacks to Ease Back into Routine

You don’t have to dive back into structure the hard way. I’ve pulled together a few scientifically backed strategies to make this transition not just seamless, but supportive of your brain and your family’s needs.

- Start the day with quiet time before the chaos
- Allow a few minutes each morning to breathe, journal, exercise, or meditate
- This mental space boosts focus and decreases stress
- Anchor new habits to existing routines
- Stack new tasks (like morning checklists or evening goal setting) with established activities such as brushing teeth or pouring coffee
- This builds habits that feel automatic over time
- Shift away from multitasking
- Focus on one task at a time to be more productive and less stressed
- It’s not about doing more; it's about doing things with intention
- Use visual calendars and planning tools
- Whether digital or paper, seeing the week mapped out helps the whole family stay on track
- This also supports clearer communication for shared responsibilities
